DREADMARCH ZONE 2 BUILD

Copy this archive's contents over the project root (~/dungeon-game).

Adds:
- The Dreadmarch wilderness zone
- Oldroot (first western town) with Inn, Training Hall, Temple, General Store
- Ironreach in the southeast as the zone's largest town shell
- Fort Dread landmark northeast of Ironreach
- Ashenwood <-> Dreadmarch transition
- Rorik Feld / Kazinov kidnapping progression
- Dreadmarch wilderness encounters
- Developer travel shortcuts

Story flow:
1. Defeat Bristle-Aptarj.
2. Report to Harlic for the Reliquary reward.
3. Return to Thornwake Town Hall. Rorik Feld explains Katy's kidnapping.
4. Press Enter during Rorik's dialogue to unlock the eastern road.
5. In Ashenwood, travel to the east-road location near Grayhaven and press Enter.
6. Enter the Dreadmarch; Oldroot is the first town.

Developer shortcuts:
F12 menu
F6 Dreadmarch
F7 Oldroot
F8 Ironreach
M  Unlock Dreadmarch / reveal Kazinov

Fort Dread is intentionally a landmark/locked content hook in this build; its dungeon interior comes next.
