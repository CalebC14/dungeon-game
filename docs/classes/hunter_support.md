# Hunter Support — Dungeon Game

**Status:** Design decisions recorded from project conversations; not proof of Godot implementation. All unspecified details are TBD, not invented.

## Locked milestones
- **L9 Hunter's Insight (passive):** Entire party +5 percentage points physical critical-hit chance, melee and ranged physical attacks only; no magic, SP, or cooldown.
- **L10 Ensnaring Trap (active):** One enemy immobilized for 2 rounds (non-boss), preventing melee attacks but still allowing ranged attacks/spells; enemy dodge −15 percentage points for 2 rounds. Bosses immune to immobilization but suffer dodge penalty. 5 SP, 4-round cooldown; no equipment requirement.
- **L20 Pack Tactics (passive):** Support animal companion automatically harasses one enemy hit by Hunter's ranged weapon attack, causing −10% physical damage dealt for 2 rounds (boss −5%). Repeated hits refresh, not stack. Multi-target actions debuff only one successfully hit enemy. No separate turn, HP, commands, SP, or cooldown. Distinct from DPS Beast Bond damage pet.
- **L30 Suppressing Volley (active):** Hit all enemies for 50% normal ranged weapon damage each, independent hit rolls; successfully hit enemies suffer −20 percentage points accuracy for 2 rounds (boss −10). Pack Tactics debuffs one successfully hit enemy. 12 SP, 5-round cooldown; ranged weapon required.
- **L40 Improved Trapping (passive):** Ensnaring Trap non-boss immobilization rises from 2 to 3 rounds, dodge penalty from −15 to −20 percentage points (including bosses, which remain immune to immobilization); Suppressing Volley accuracy penalty rises from −20 to −25 percentage points on regular enemies, −10 to −12 on bosses. Original dodge/accuracy debuff durations remain 2 rounds. No SP/cooldown.
- **L50 Bear Trap (active):** **Supersedes name/theme Explosive Trap**. Magically summoned bear manifests, attacks all enemies, then disappears; 75% normal ranged weapon damage per enemy; independent 50% stun chance for 1 round on non-bosses (stunned loses next action). Bosses cannot be stunned; instead suffer −20% physical damage dealt for 2 rounds. Pack Tactics debuffs one successfully hit enemy. 18 SP, 7-round cooldown; ranged weapon required. Bear is a temporary manifestation, not a second permanent companion.
- **L60 Pack Ambush (active):** Magical animal pack attacks all enemies, reducing enemy armor/defense by 25% for 3 rounds; grants whole party +15 percentage points physical crit chance for 3 rounds. **Both effects apply before arrows.** Hunter fires crippling arrows equal to current attack count; each arrow independently targets and rolls hit, dealing 100% normal ranged weapon damage. Each successful arrow stuns non-boss enemy for 1 round; bosses instead suffer −20% physical damage for 2 rounds. Multiple arrows on same enemy refresh rather than stack stun. Pack Tactics applies its usual debuff to one successfully hit enemy. 30 SP, 10-round cooldown; ranged weapon required.

## Open implementation details
How Pack Ambush's party +15 percentage-point crit buff stacks with Hunter's Insight +5 is not separately specified; use shared buff-stacking rules when defined. For Bear Trap, precise hit-resolution rules and whether the magical bear's attack is considered a ranged weapon hit for all other triggers require implementation clarification; do not invent these rules.
